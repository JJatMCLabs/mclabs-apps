Stitchwork

A cozy sewing game. Customers bring you a wish, and you design, cut and sew it, then watch them try it on.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • DesignMix necklines, collars, sleeves, bodices and skirts. Every piece fits together.
  • CutLay the pattern on the fabric, choose where the print lands, and trace each line with the shears.
  • SewPin each piece on the dress form and stitch the seam with your finger.
  • DecorateButtons, bows, lace, patches and pockets, or embroider a daisy in the hoop.
  • Show it offPick a pose and a set, and save a photo of every piece you make.
  • Gentle by designNo ads, no timers, no fails. Plays offline, with no account.

What's in it

Three chapters and 30 customer jobs, dresses, tops and skirts, 47 fabrics, 40 decorations and 24 thread colours. The first chapter is free. One purchase unlocks chapters 2 and 3, plus tops and skirts, for good.
