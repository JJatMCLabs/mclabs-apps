Castaway Lighthouse

A cozy island village builder. Wash ashore with three friends, turn a shipwreck camp into a village, and light the old lighthouse again.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • BuildPaint a hut's floor with your finger and watch the walls rise. Palm huts, bamboo cabins, plank houses and stone cottages, in any shape.
  • Peek insideLift the roof to look in, furnish the rooms, and rebuild them into better homes as the island gives more.
  • Look after everyoneYour castaways gather wood, fronds, stone and clay, cook at the fire, eat together and sleep in their own beds.
  • Welcome newcomersEach one has a story, a wish and a bed they need before they move in.
  • Light the lighthouseSalvage the wreck, meet the hermit of the grove, and light the beam for a festival in the bay.
  • Gentle by designNo ads, no timers, no energy to wait for. Plays offline, with no account.

What's in it

A story for eight castaways across five chapters, four styles of home, and a whole island to settle. Everything up to your first village of five is free. One purchase, "The whole island", unlocks the rest of the story for good: three more castaways and the lighthouse festival. Family Sharing is supported.
